## 1. Template A: Executive Growth Brief (C-Level / VP Marketing)

```markdown
# 📈 Organic Growth Intelligence Brief: [Target Domain]
**Date**: [YYYY-MM-DD] | **Target Market**: [US / Global] | **Prepared By**: Ahrefs SEO Intelligence Engine

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Market Position
- **Current Standing**: [Target Domain] captures **[X] monthly visits** with an estimated PPC replacement value of **$[Y]/mo**.
- **Competitive Authority Gap**: At **DR [A]**, the domain lags behind market leader [Competitor Domain] (**DR [B]**) by **[B - A] authority points**.
- **Top Growth Lever**: Capturing **[N] striking-distance keywords** (positions 4–15) represents an immediate **$[Z]/mo** organic value unlock.

---

### 2. Market Leader Benchmark Scorecard
| Dimension | Target Domain | Competitor 1 | Competitor 2 | Variance | Strategic Opportunity |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---|
| **Domain Rating (DR)** | 48 | 68 | 62 | -20 pts | High-DR digital PR link outreach required. |
| **Referring Domains** | 420 | 2,150 | 1,840 | -1,730 RDs | Competitor shares 112 unlinked industry PR donors. |
| **Organic Traffic** | 35,000 | 240,000 | 180,000 | -205,000 | 85% traffic gap concentrated in BoFu category terms. |
| **Traffic Value ($)** | $28,500 | $310,000 | $195,000 | -$281,500 | Massive paid acquisition savings potential. |

---

### 3. Three-Horizon Action Roadmap
- **P0 (Sprint 1 — Weeks 1–3)**: Optimize 8 striking-distance URLs with intent sharpening & internal linking.
- **P1 (Quarterly — Months 1–3)**: Produce 4 dedicated commercial comparison pages (`/vs/` alternatives).
- **P2 (Strategic Moat — Months 2–6)**: Target top 20 intersecting high-DR referring domains for digital PR.
```

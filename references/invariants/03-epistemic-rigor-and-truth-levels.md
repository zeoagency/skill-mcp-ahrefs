# Epistemic Rigor & Truth Levels in SEO Intelligence

## 1. The Epistemic Law: Truth Separation

A premier SEO Intelligence Agent must never conflate raw observations with derived calculations, strategic interpretations, or hypothetical recommendations. In high-stakes organic growth advisory, presenting an unverified assumption as an empirical fact damages credibility and leads to misallocated engineering resources.

Every analytical statement generated by this skill belongs to exactly one of five distinct **Truth Levels**:

```
[Level 1: Observation] ──► [Level 2: Calculation] ──► [Level 3: Interpretation] ──► [Level 4: Hypothesis] ──► [Level 5: Recommendation]
(Raw Empirical Data)      (Deterministic Math)        (Domain Context)             (Testable Claim)            (Actionable Next Step)
```

---

## 2. The Five Truth Levels

### Level 1: Empirical Observation (Raw Tool Fact)
- **Definition**: Unaltered, verified data points directly returned by the upstream tool or extracted from an artifact.
- **Characteristics**: Directly citable, zero inference, objective.
- **Examples**:
  - `ahrefs_site_overview` returned `domainRating: 54` and `orgTraffic: 48200`.
  - `ahrefs_organic_keywords` observed keyword `"crm software"` at `position: 8` with `volume: 14000` in the US database.
  - `ahrefs_backlinks` returned 12 referring domains for the URL `/pricing`.

### Level 2: Deterministic Calculation (Mathematical Derivative)
- **Definition**: Exact mathematical results computed strictly from Level 1 observations.
- **Characteristics**: Reproducible, transparent formula, zero speculation.
- **Examples**:
  - Traffic gap between Target ($48,200$) and Competitor ($185,000$) is $136,800$ monthly visits ($74.0\%$ deficit).
  - Striking Distance Opportunity Score (SDOS): $\text{SDOS} = \frac{14000 \times 4.5}{8 \times (24 + 1)} = 315.0$.
  - Competitor link overlap: 4 out of 5 competitors share referring domain `techcrunch.com` ($80\%$ consensus).

### Level 3: Strategic Interpretation (Domain Understanding)
- **Definition**: Contextual meaning assigned to observations and calculations based on SEO and marketing principles.
- **Characteristics**: Evaluative, grounded in domain heuristics, clearly stated as an interpretation.
- **Examples**:
  - Ranking at position 8 represents Page 1 visibility on the cusp of the click-through cliff (striking distance).
  - A Domain Rating of 54 against an industry median of 68 indicates an authority deficit that restricts competitiveness for high-KD keywords ($KD > 50$).
  - An organic keyword footprint where $85\%$ of traffic derives from brand variations indicates acute vulnerability to generic category competitors.

### Level 4: Testable Hypothesis (Causal Proposition)
- **Definition**: A reasoned explanation or proposed mechanism for an observed outcome, formulated to be tested against further evidence.
- **Characteristics**: Conditional ("if/then"), probabilistic, falsifiable.
- **Examples**:
  - *Hypothesis*: The recent traffic plateau on `/blog/saas-metrics` is caused by search intent shifting toward interactive benchmark calculators rather than static definitions.
  - *Hypothesis*: Consolidating three cannibalizing subpages (`/email-guide`, `/email-tips`, `/email-best-practices`) into a single 301-redirected pillar will consolidate link equity and lift rankings from position 11 to top 3.

### Level 5: Actionable Recommendation (Intervention)
- **Definition**: A concrete, prioritized strategic initiative proposed to stakeholders, paired with expected impact, required effort, and evidence basis.
- **Characteristics**: Directive, time-boxed (P0/P1/P2), business-aligned.
- **Examples**:
  - *P0 Sprint*: Add internal links from top-traffic pages `/integrations` and `/features` pointing to `/pricing` using anchor `"enterprise crm pricing"`.
  - *P1 Quarterly*: Produce an exhaustive `/vs/hubspot` comparison page targeting commercial search intent ($1,200$ vol, $KD: 18$, $CPC: \$12.50$).

## 3. Disclosed Regional Assumptions

When analyzing geographic markets, the agent must avoid geographic fallacies:

1. **US is Not Global**:
   - The US keyword database (`country: "us"`) is a country-specific sample of American search demand.
   - It must never be presented as a proxy for worldwide or European demand. If a worldwide evaluation is requested, organic metrics must use `country: undefined` (omitted) or multi-country batch analysis.
2. **ccTLDs Do Not Prove Total Audience**:
   - A country-code top-level domain (e.g., `.de`, `.com.tr`, `.co.uk`) indicates a primary market focus or hosting origin.
   - It does not prove that the business lacks international traffic or that its audience is exclusively located in that country. State regional focus as a disclosed working assumption, not an absolute proof.

## 4. Negative Assertions & Population Limits

When inspecting bounded results:

- **Absence in Capture $\neq$ Absence in Index**:
  - If a 100-row capture from `ahrefs_organic_keywords` contains 0 transactional keywords, report:
    > "Zero transactional keywords were observed within the top 100 captured organic queries."
  - Never state: "The domain has zero transactional keywords in Google."
- **Filtered Emptiness $\neq$ Unranked Domain**:
  - If `ahrefs_organic_keywords(minVolume: 10000)` returns 0 rows, check `retrievalMetadata`. If 100 rows were captured and all 100 had volume $< 10000$, report:
    > "100 keywords were captured, but all had monthly search volume below the requested 10,000 threshold. Relaxing the filter to 1,000 reveals 28 ranking terms."
